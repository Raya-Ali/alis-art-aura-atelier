Restoration & Aftercare

Preserve the artwork, memories and meaningful pieces you already own. From restoration and repair to preservation, presentation and ongoing care, we help give treasured pieces a chance to be appreciated again.

What We Can Help With

Restoration begins with understanding what the piece needs. Every artwork, frame and object can respond differently to cleaning, repair and restoration, so projects are assessed individually.

Artwork Restoration

Assistance with damaged, aged or worn artwork, including assessment of visible deterioration and possible restoration approaches.

Frame Restoration

Refreshing, repairing or improving the presentation of frames that have become worn, damaged or unsuitable for the artwork.

Surface & Finish Care

Care and preservation considerations for artwork surfaces, finishes, decorative elements and presentation materials.

Damage Assessment

A closer review of visible damage to help determine whether restoration, preservation, repair or recreation is the most appropriate direction.

Preservation

Guidance and presentation choices designed to help reduce unnecessary exposure to damaging environmental conditions.

Recreation

When an original piece cannot reasonably be restored, recreation or artistic reproduction may provide another way to preserve its meaning.

Presentation Protection

Advice on framing, display and protective presentation for pieces that need additional care after restoration.

Aftercare Guidance

Practical guidance for caring for your artwork or restored piece after the project is completed.

The Restoration Process

Because restoration can involve different materials and levels of damage, the project begins with assessment before any major work is recommended.

STEP 01

Review

We review the piece, its condition, materials and the concerns you have about its current appearance or structure.

STEP 02

Assessment

Visible damage and restoration requirements are considered before determining what work may be appropriate.

STEP 03

Recommendation

You receive a recommended direction, which may involve restoration, repair, preservation, recreation or a combination.

STEP 04

Quotation

A custom quotation is prepared based on the piece, materials, complexity and requested work.

STEP 05

Restoration

Once the project is approved, the agreed work can begin according to the confirmed scope.

STEP 06

Aftercare

Final care and presentation guidance help you understand how to maintain the piece after completion.

Aftercare & Preservation

  • Keep artwork away from prolonged direct sunlight when possible.
  • Avoid excessive moisture and environments with persistent humidity.
  • Keep pieces away from direct heat sources.
  • Handle artwork with clean, dry hands when appropriate.
  • Avoid using household cleaning products directly on artwork.
  • Do not attempt aggressive cleaning or repair without understanding the materials involved.
  • Use appropriate framing and protective presentation for valuable or delicate pieces.
  • Seek professional assessment when damage, mold, staining or structural problems are present.
