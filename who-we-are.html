Alis Art & Aura Atelier

Who We Are

An atelier built around the belief that art can carry more than beauty. It can carry words, memories, emotions, identity and meaning.

The Atelier

Alis Art & Aura Atelier is a creative space where different forms of art, design and expression can exist together.

We create with the understanding that people do not always buy things simply because they are beautiful. Sometimes they are looking for something that means something.

Our Approach

We begin with meaning and allow the creative form to follow.

A memory can become artwork. A message can become a keepsake. An idea can become a design. A feeling can become something tangible.

Our Vision

We imagine a creative world without one box. A place where artists, designers, makers and other creative minds can contribute their own abilities while people discover meaningful creations in one place.

The Atelier is being built with that larger possibility in mind.

What We Believe

Meaning

The things we create become more meaningful when they carry something of us.

Originality

Creativity should have room to be personal, unexpected and uniquely expressed.

Possibility

A creative idea does not always have to fit an existing category.
