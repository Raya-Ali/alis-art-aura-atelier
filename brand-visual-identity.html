Brand & Visual Identity

A visual identity gives an idea a recognizable presence. We create thoughtful visual systems that help businesses, creators and personal brands express who they are through design, presentation and visual language.

What We Create

More Than A Logo

A brand identity is the visual foundation of how an idea is experienced. Depending on what you need, your project can include a single visual element or an entire identity system.

Logo Design

Primary logos, alternate logos, symbols, monograms and visual marks created around your brand concept.

Colour Direction

A considered colour palette that gives your brand consistency across digital and physical applications.

Typography

Font and lettering direction selected to help communicate your brand's personality and message.

Visual Language

Shapes, imagery, patterns, textures and other visual elements that make your identity recognizable.

Brand Materials

Business cards, social graphics, promotional materials, stationery and other branded pieces.

Brand Guidelines

A reference system showing how your visual identity should be used consistently across different materials.
